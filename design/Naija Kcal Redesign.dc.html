1Naija Kcal — structural redesign

The current build is Cal AI's funnel with a scotch-bonnet repaint. The thesis of this pass: the differentiator isn't the dish database, it's the unit of logging. Cal AI logs one photographed item. Nigerian eating is a pair — soup and swallow — served from a shared bowl, measured in wraps, dericas and ladles. So the app is rebuilt around a pair logger with local units and a share-of-bowl step, and the 20-screen funnel is cut to 9 by earning trust with a working scan before asking for anything.
